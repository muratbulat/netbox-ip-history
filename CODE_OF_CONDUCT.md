# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, using welcoming language, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, trolling, public or private abuse, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainer or GitHub's available reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly. This project follows the Contributor Covenant principles; see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ for the full reference.
